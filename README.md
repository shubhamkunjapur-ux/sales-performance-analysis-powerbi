# 📌 Project Overview

This project focuses on **Sales Performance Analysis and Advanced KPI Analysis** using Excel and Microsoft Power BI.

The objective is to analyze sales data, calculate important business KPIs, identify performance trends, and create an interactive dashboard that can support data-driven business decision-making.

The project combines:

- Excel-based data analysis
- KPI calculations
- Sales and profit analysis
- Trend analysis
- Data visualization
- Power BI dashboard development
- Interactive filters and slicers
- Business insights

---

# 🎯 Project Objectives

The major objectives of this project are:

- Analyze overall sales performance.
- Calculate and monitor important business KPIs.
- Analyze sales and profitability.
- Identify sales trends over time.
- Compare performance across regions.
- Analyze product and category performance.
- Identify important business patterns.
- Create an interactive Power BI dashboard.
- Present business insights through clear visualizations.

# 📊 Excel Analysis

The Excel workbook contains multiple analytical components.

### 📄 Sales Data

The `Sales Data` sheet contains the underlying sales dataset used for analysis.

Key fields include:

- Order ID
- Order Date
- Region
- Product
- Category
- Sales
- Profit
- Quantity

---

### 📈 KPI Summary

The `KPI Summary` sheet provides a business-level overview of important sales performance indicators.

It is designed to provide a quick snapshot of business performance and support management-level analysis.

---

### 📊 Charts

The `Charts` sheet contains visual elements used for understanding sales performance and trends.

---

# 📈 Power BI Dashboard

The Power BI dashboard converts the sales analysis into an interactive business intelligence report.

The dashboard provides a visual and interactive way to explore sales performance and business KPIs.

---

## 📌 Key Analysis Areas

### 1. KPI Analysis

The dashboard focuses on important business performance indicators such as:

- Total Sales
- Total Profit
- Quantity
- Profitability
- Sales Performance

---

### 2. Sales Trend Analysis

Sales performance is analyzed over time to identify:

- Growth patterns
- Declining periods
- Sales fluctuations
- Overall trends

---

### 3. Regional Performance

Sales and profitability can be compared across different regions to understand regional business performance.

---

### 4. Product Performance

The analysis helps identify products contributing to sales and profitability and allows comparison between different products.

---

### 5. Category Performance

Product categories can be analyzed to understand their contribution to overall business performance.

---

### 6. Profitability Analysis

Profit-related KPIs and visualizations help evaluate the relationship between sales and profit and identify stronger and weaker performing segments.

---

# 🎛️ Interactive Dashboard Features

The Power BI dashboard provides interactive reporting capabilities through:

- Slicers
- Filters
- Interactive charts
- KPI cards
- Trend visualizations
- Cross-filtering
- Business performance comparisons

These features allow users to explore the data from different perspectives.
